My Story

Usairim Kamal Azfar Wakeel

My name is Usairim Kamal Azfar Wakeel. I am 9 years old, and I am from Pakistan.

My journey started with gaming. I used to play games a lot and enjoy exploring different levels and challenges. At first, it was just for fun.

But after some time, I started thinking differently. I began asking how games are made, how characters move, and how everything works behind the screen.

That’s when I became interested in coding.

I started learning HTML and CSS. It was not easy at the beginning. Sometimes my code didn’t work, and I had to fix many mistakes. But I kept trying.

Then I learned JavaScript. That made things more exciting because I could finally add logic and movement to my projects.

After learning the basics, I started building my own projects. One of them is Usairim Web Tech.

It is where I create and improve my coding projects and games. It helps me test ideas and learn new things every day.

Now, I don’t just play games anymore—I build them.

I enjoy solving problems, trying new ideas, and turning code into real working projects. I am still learning, but I keep improving step by step.

My journey started as a gamer, then became curiosity, and now I am building my own games.

This is just the beginning of my journey in coding.
