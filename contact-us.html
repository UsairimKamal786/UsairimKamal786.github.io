FAQ

Common Questions

How do I report a DMCA / copyright issue? +
Select the "DMCA" category in the contact form above and describe the content in question. Include links and ownership proof. We aim to respond within 72 hours.
I have a business / sponsorship inquiry. +
Choose the "Business" or "Advertise" category and provide your company details and proposal. Our partnership team will reach out within 48 hours.
A game download isn't working. What should I do? +
Select "Support" in the form above and mention the game title and the error you're seeing. Alternatively, the fastest help is in our Discord's #support channel.
Can I submit my game to be featured on the site? +
Absolutely! Use the "Business" category and include your game name, description, platform, and any press kit. We review all submissions.
How do I get my account unbanned? +
Use the "Support" category and provide your username and the reason you believe the ban was a mistake. Account reviews take up to 5 business days.
What are your moderation / community rules? +
Full community guidelines are available on the Community Guidelines page. Short version: be respectful, no spam or hate speech, follow Discord's TOS.
